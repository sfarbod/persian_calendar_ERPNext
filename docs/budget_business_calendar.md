# Budget & Monthly Distribution — Business Calendar Integration (Phase 3b)

## Installed ERPNext v16 reality check

In ERPNext **v16.29**, Budget **no longer uses Monthly Distribution**.

| Concern | v16 behavior |
|---------|----------------|
| Budget periods | Generated by `Budget.get_budget_periods()`, stored on **Budget Distribution** child (`start_date`, `end_date`, `amount`, `percent`) |
| Monthly Distribution | Global template (no company). Used by **Sales Person / Territory / Partner target** reports only |
| Accumulation checks | Query stored Budget Distribution rows by `start_date` — no re-generation at spend time |

## Extension mechanisms

| Target | Mechanism | Why |
|--------|-----------|-----|
| `Budget.get_budget_periods` | `override_doctype_class` → `PersianCalendarBudget` | Instance method; no free-function patch |
| `MonthlyDistribution.get_months` | `override_doctype_class` → `PersianCalendarMonthlyDistribution` | Seeds English Jan–Dec labels (unchanged) |
| `get_periodwise_distribution_data` / `get_percentage` | Central `apply_calendar_patches()` | Free functions; identity rebinding for importers |

## Calendar ownership

- **Budget:** `Budget.company` → Business Calendar.
- **Monthly Distribution:** calendar-neutral template. Consuming report company / period keys select matching mode.
- Same January–December document may be reused by Gregorian and Jalali companies:
  - Position `idx` 1–12 is the business month slot.
  - Gregorian math matches English month names (stock).
  - Jalali math matches `Period.period_number` ↔ `idx` (Farvardin = 1, even if the row label is still “January”).

## Gregorian compatibility

`PersianCalendarBudget.get_budget_periods()` calls `super()` for Gregorian companies — exact stock ERPNext behavior including `get_first_day` snapping.

## Jalali Budget periods

Uses `BusinessPeriodEngine.generate(..., provider=JalaliCalendarProvider)`:

- Farvardin–Shahrivar: 31-day Gregorian storage spans
- Mehr–Bahman: 30-day
- Esfand: 29 / 30 (leap)
- Quarterly / Half-Yearly / Yearly aligned to Jalali seasons

Stored dates remain Gregorian. Display Calendar never participates.

## Historical / submitted documents

- Periods are **stored** on save when distribution regenerates.
- Submitted Budgets are not rewritten by this app.
- Changing Company Business Calendar does **not** migrate old rows; draft/amend regenerates via stock `before_save` when frequency/FY/amount changes.
- Default Business Calendar remains Gregorian → zero change for existing sites.

## Allocation precision

Equal split still uses stock `Budget.add_allocated_amount` → `flt(..., 3)`. Only period boundaries change for Jalali.

## Phase 3c preview

Budget Variance / `controllers.trends.get_period_date_ranges` should consume `BusinessPeriodEngine` next — not reimplement month arithmetic.
